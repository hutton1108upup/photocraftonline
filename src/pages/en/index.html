PhotoCraft Online FAQ

Is PhotoCraft online free to use?

Yes. The hosted editor is free to use. Our PNG import, layer edit and export test had no payment step.

Do I need an account?

No site account is required to open the editor or follow the PNG tutorial.

Can I use PhotoCraft without downloading a desktop app?

Yes. Use the browser editor on a supported desktop browser. The first visit downloads the editor assets, including about 25 MB of WebAssembly.

Which file formats were tested here?

Our beginner workflow tested PNG editing and saving and reopening a simple two-layer PSD. Complex PSDs, text, masks and Photoshop round trips still need separate checks.

Where can I download PhotoCraft for desktop?

Use our download page to choose Windows, Mac or Linux assets from the official GitHub release.

Why is PhotoCraft not loading in my browser?

Startup depends on graphics support and successful editor-asset downloads. See loading help for browser and network checks.

Does this site upload my image?

No image-upload request was observed in our tested PNG editing workflow. This site has no image-upload endpoint. See the privacy policy for hosting requests, local storage and external services.

Is FreePhotoCraft.online the official PhotoCraft website?

No. This is an independent website hosting a PhotoCraft WebAssembly build and guides. It is not operated by or affiliated with ArtCraft.
