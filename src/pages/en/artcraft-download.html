ArtCraft AI: image, video and canvas creation

For the AI creative studio, use the official ArtCraft download page for Windows or macOS, or open the official web app. Its quick start includes creating an account or signing in.

The official page lists Windows 10 64-bit or newer and macOS 12 or newer, with 8 GB RAM recommended and 2 GB available storage. It does not offer a Linux option on that page; do not transfer the Craft apps’ platform list to this product.

A free software download does not establish unlimited free AI generation. Check official pricing and allowances before using generation services.

ArtCraft Launcher: manage several Craft apps

Upstream presents the Launcher as a way to install, open and update multiple apps. Open the official Launcher release, expand Assets and choose the package matching your system and architecture. Follow the Launcher documentation, then select the apps you need. The Launcher and individual apps have separate versions.

We have not tested Launcher installation or updates and do not supply a third-party bundled installer.

Pick an installer, not the wrong ZIP

  • MSI installs a Windows app. Portable ZIP runs after extraction; keep its companion files together.
  • DMG contains a Mac desktop app. Match Linux packages to your processor and distribution.
  • Web ZIP contains files for self-hosting, not a desktop installer.
  • Source code ZIP usually needs building. Ordinary users should choose compiled release assets.
