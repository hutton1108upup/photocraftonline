What to check yourself

JPEG input, complex PSDs, smart objects, adjustment layers, plug-ins, print color workflows and Photoshop round trips do not have a completed test record here. Keep an original copy, open a small representative file first, save an editable document, export a flattened image, and reopen both outputs.
